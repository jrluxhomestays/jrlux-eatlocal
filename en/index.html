Where to Eat Like a Local

Recommended By Your Host

These are not Michelin-graded or starred places. They are recommended by your host for local taste and good food. Twenty places we like for local food, from Malay restaurants to simple kopitiams (traditional coffee shops for breakfast and everyday local dishes), grouped by halal status. Tap a name to open it.

Please note: Muslim friendly is our host's note that a place does not serve pork or lard. It is not a halal certification, so please confirm with the staff if it matters to you. We do not certify any outlet. Opening hours, menus and prices change, so check before you go.
